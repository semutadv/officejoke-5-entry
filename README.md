One office joke, written for the make.fun bounty "The Funniest Office
Joke", judged head-to-head. Every company proudly announces an
open-door policy; every meeting, without exception, begins with
somebody getting up to close the door. Both facts are true, and the
second one is the policy that ships.

## Running it

`node main.ts` clears the output directory's contents and writes the
line to `/output/entry.txt` as plain UTF-8 text.
